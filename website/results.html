Results

Exploratory feasibility study -- not a completed gene-attribution study

Real-data run (2026-11-23): preliminary feasibility check, not a finished analysis. A real subset of DANDI:000636 (human ephys) and real Allen Cell Types Database mouse ephys data were downloaded and processed -- see docs/DATA_MANIFEST.md. The human ephys-only cross-species comparison arm ran on this real data, but on only 4 usable human cells (summary below) -- treat these numbers as preliminary only; they do not demonstrate a reliable species difference. The mouse gene-attribution arm did not run on real data and no mouse genes have been identified as candidates: no real, properly licensed, cell-linked mouse transcriptome was available (the NeMO transcriptome bundle currently shows no license and was not downloaded, per this project's own stop condition). Nothing on this page is causal.

Caveat: n=4 real human cells -- preliminary only, no reliable species difference demonstrated. Of 20 downloaded DANDI:000636 NWB files, only 4 yielded ephys features that ipfx's default long-square detection could extract. The other 16 failed because their stimulus-naming convention isn't recognized by the extractor's default configuration -- this is described here as an unresolved pipeline limitation, not a claim that those 16 recordings are unusable by any possible method; a different extraction approach may well recover them. With n=4, these per-species means are not a statistically powered comparison, no significance claim is made, and they should not be read as evidence of a real mouse-human difference. Mouse gene-attribution (ridge/lasso/reduced-rank regression, stability selection) was not run on real data and no mouse genes have been identified as candidates -- see results/real/summary.json's arms_blocked field.
